How to play

Guess the mystery FC 26 player. After each guess, every attribute is checked against the answer.

  • CB Match — that attribute is correct.
  • MID Close — same position group, or a nearby age/rating.
  • ST No match.

For Age and Rating, an arrow shows whether the answer is higher (↑) or lower (↓) than your guess.

Daily Match gives everyone the same player for the day. Practice pulls a fresh random player from the database every round, with no limit on how many times you play.
